## 💻 My Problem-Solving Strategy

I'm not following a solution-copying approach.

For every topic, I want to follow a structured learning process:

1. **Understand the Concept** — Learn the fundamentals and how the technique works.
2. **Implement in Java** — Write the implementation myself.
3. **Solve Easy Problems** — Start with straightforward problems to understand the concept.
4. **Recognize Patterns** — Identify when and why a particular technique is useful.
5. **Practice Independently** — Attempt problems without looking at editorials or solutions.
6. **Analyze Mistakes** — Understand why my approach failed and how to improve it.
7. **Revisit Problems** — Re-solve previously completed problems to strengthen retention.

My priority is to understand the logic behind a solution rather than simply getting an Accepted verdict.
